Until now only a company's admin could start a Live Chat conversation. Now any operator can open one with any company from the Organizations page, for example to help with onboarding, remind a company that its plan ends soon, or follow up on a suggestion. This page explains how, what the company sees and when to use it.

## What it does
On the Organizations page, each row has a **Chat** icon in its Actions cell. Clicking it opens the support chat for that company, in the floating chat panel or on the full Live Chat page. If the company already has a conversation, you see its history. If it has none, you see an empty conversation with the reply box ready.

Opening the chat creates nothing. The conversation is created when you send the first message. If you close the panel without sending, the company never knows.

## Start a conversation
Who can do this: any operator. It is the same permission as replying to a chat.

1. Open the **Organizations** page and find the company (search by name, email, mobile, state, district or pincode).
2. Click the **Chat** icon in the **Actions** cell of its row. The chat opens with the company name and the admin's name, email and mobile at the top.
3. If the conversation is closed, click **Reopen** first. A closed conversation hides the reply box.
4. Type in **Write a reply** and press Enter to send. A message cannot be empty and can be at most 4000 characters. You can also attach a file or picture of up to 10 MB with the paperclip.
5. The company now appears in the conversation list, and from here the chat works like any other: unread counts, the bell, closing and reopening. See [Live Chat with companies](kb:live-chat).

## What the company sees
The company's admin gets a phone notification when push is set up on their device, and the message appears in the app under **Support > Live Chat**. They reply there. The conversation is not tied to a plan, so a company whose plan has lapsed can still read and answer it. The system does not hold messages back until morning, so a message you send at night reaches their phone at night.

## When to start a chat
- A **renewal reminder** for a company whose plan ends soon (see the Subscriptions expiring card on the dashboard).
- **Onboarding help** for a new company that has not added staff or branches yet.
- A **payment problem** you found, for example a plan that did not activate after payment.
- A **follow-up** on a suggestion, when a short chat is faster than waiting.

## When not to start a chat
- **Marketing or bulk messages.** Live Chat is for support. One company, one reason.
- Anything that needs a **decision a person in chat cannot make**, such as a refund or a price exception. Check with the owner first, then write.
- **Sensitive requests.** Never ask for a password or a one-time code, and never ask the admin to share one.

## What to say first
State who you are and why you are writing, in two or three sentences, and make the next step easy. For example: "Hello, this is Clockyn support. Your plan ends on the 30th. Would you like help renewing it?" Use the company's name or the admin's name. If the chat leads to an important decision or a problem you cannot solve, note it in the chat so the history stays in one place. See [Live Chat](kb:live-chat).

## What can go wrong
- **The company is deleted (404):** the console answers 404, as the organisation is not found, and nothing is created. A deleted company cannot be chatted with.
- **The company has no active admin (409):** the console shows "This organization has no active admin to chat with". There is nobody to receive the message. Check the company's users, or contact the owner by phone or email.
- **The Chat icon opens an empty conversation:** that is normal for a company that never wrote. Nothing exists until you send the first message.
- **No reply:** the admin may have notifications off or may not open the app. Try a phone call or email from the contact details at the top of the chat.
- **File refused:** files over 10 MB are blocked.

## Common questions
### Does opening the chat notify the company?
No. Only sending a message does.

### Can a manager or staff member receive the message?
No. Live Chat is for the company's Admin only, so the message goes to the admin.

### Can I start a chat with a company whose plan has expired?
Yes. Live Chat is available whatever the plan status.

### Can I start chats in bulk?
No. Each chat is started one company at a time from that company's row, which suits the one-company-one-reason rule above.

### Who sees what I wrote?
Every operator can read every conversation, and the company's admins see it in their app. Write as if both will read it.

### I started a chat by mistake. Can I undo it?
You can delete your message (the three-dot menu, **Delete message**), which removes it from the company's app too. You can then close the conversation. The notification may already have reached their phone.
