This checklist turns the console into a routine. The daily part takes about fifteen minutes when nothing is wrong. It follows the order in which things become urgent: customers waiting for an answer first, money problems second, housekeeping last. Adapt it to your team's own rules; the console only shows what is on screen and does not message customers by itself.

## Start of every day

Who can do this: any operator, unless a step says super admin.

1. **Open the bell.** Click the bell in the top bar. If it lists **Live chat**, **Account deletion requests** or **Feature suggestions**, those are your first jobs. If it says "You're all caught up" move on. Reload the page first if you have had it open for a long time, because only the chat count updates by itself.
2. **Answer live chats.** Click the round chat button at the bottom right and reply to unread conversations, oldest first. See [Live Chat](kb:live-chat).
3. **Check the Dashboard.** Open [Dashboard](kb:dashboard). Look at the tiles for Organizations, Users, Revenue (Month) and Active Plans, and the **Active Today** card. A sudden drop in Active Today during working hours is worth a question to the developers.
4. **Account deletion requests.** If the number next to **Users** in the left menu is above zero, open the Account Deletion Requests page (see [Account deletion requests](kb:account-deletion-requests)). Deleting an account is permanent and asks for an e-mail code, so only process a request after your team's verification steps are done.
5. **Tickets.** Open Ticketing and Bugs, set the status filter to **Open** and handle the oldest first; move a ticket to **In Progress** when you start and **Resolved** when it is done. See [Ticketing and Bugs](kb:tickets-and-bugs).
6. **Suggestions.** Skim new feature requests and reply where a reply is useful. See [Suggestions](kb:suggestions).
7. **Payments that need attention.** Open Payments and filter by **Failed** and **Pending**. Also look at **Created** payments that are old: the customer started to pay but never finished. See [Payments](kb:payments).
8. **Subscriptions about to end.** On the Dashboard, read the **Subscriptions expiring** card (Today, Next 7 days, Next 30 days). Click **Today** and then **Next 7 days**: the Organizations page opens with the Expiring filter on, soonest end date first, and shows exactly that many companies. Follow your team's process to contact each owner before the plan ends. The Subscriptions page also marks a plan that ends within 15 days with an orange **Expiring** badge. See [Dashboard](kb:dashboard) and [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

> **Tip** Write down anything unusual (an organisation with many failed payments, a chat that needs a developer) and hand it over at the end of the day.

## End of every week

1. **Referrals and commission (super admin).** Open Referrals, tab **B2C Referral**, and read the **Commission due** figures. Pay promoters according to your agreement and record each transfer with **Record payout**. See [Commission and payouts](kb:b2c-commission-and-payouts).
2. **Referral activity.** On the **C2C Referral** tab look at the number of referrals and how many have paid. See [C2C referrals](kb:referrals-c2c).
3. **Renewals for the month ahead.** On the Dashboard click **Next 30 days** in the Subscriptions expiring card and plan outreach for the companies that are not in the 7-day list yet.
4. **Payments review.** Open Payments, look at **Success Rate** and the **Failed** and **Pending** tiles for the week, and export the list (Excel) if finance asked for it.
5. **How people use the app.** Open [Clickstream](kb:clickstream) and note which screens are used most and where people drop off. Share findings with the product team.
6. **Help content.** Open [Video Views](kb:video-views) to see which guide videos people open. If many questions arrive on a topic, ask a super admin to add or improve an FAQ in [FAQ Hub](kb:faq-hub).
7. **Coupons.** Open [Coupons](kb:coupons) and switch off coupons that are no longer meant to be used.

## Start of every month

1. **Payments export for finance.** Export Payments for the month (Excel or PDF) and check the **Total Revenue** tile against the Dashboard's **Revenue (Month)**.
2. **Plan catalogue check.** Open Subscriptions, section **Available plans**, and confirm that the prices, durations, free months and offer texts are what the business expects.
3. **Console users (super admin).** Open Settings, Users and remove people who have left the team. See [Settings page](kb:settings-page).
4. **Organisation status.** On Organizations, filter by **Expired** and **Free Trial** to see who may need a follow-up. The **Chat** icon on a row lets you message the company's admin; see [Start a chat with a company](kb:start-a-chat-with-a-company).

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A number differs between Dashboard and Payments | They use different filters or time windows | Compare the same period; see the notes on each page |
| The bell is empty but a customer says they wrote | The bell lists only live chat, deletion requests and suggestions | Check Tickets and the Live Chat page |
| Chat count not changing | The chat connection may be down; the status in the chat panel shows Reconnecting | See [Troubleshooting](kb:troubleshooting) |
| You cannot record a payout | Only super admins can | Ask a super admin |

## Common questions

### How long should the daily check take?
About fifteen minutes on a quiet day. Chats and tickets are what take longer.

### Which task is the most urgent?
A customer waiting in Live Chat, then a failed payment from a customer who is trying to buy, then expiring subscriptions.

### Do I need to do everything every day?
Do steps 1 to 5 every day. Steps 6 to 8 can be done once a day at a fixed time. The weekly and monthly lists are for the end of the period.

### What do I hand over at the end of the day?
Anything you did not finish: open chats, tickets in progress, organisations you were going to contact.

### I am new. Where do I start?
Read [Welcome](kb:welcome) and the [Console tour](kb:console-tour), then follow this list once with a colleague.
