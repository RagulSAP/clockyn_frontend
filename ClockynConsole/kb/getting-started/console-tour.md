This page walks through the whole console screen so that you know what every control does before you open a single customer. Once you know the layout, every page behaves in a familiar way.

## The left menu

The left menu (the sidebar) is the same on every page. It has the Clockyn logo at the top, three groups of links, and your name with a log-out icon at the bottom. The page you are on is highlighted.

| Group | Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Dashboard | Live numbers across all organisations, who is active today, a revenue chart and how many paid plans end today, within 7 days and within 30 days (click one to see the companies) |
| Management | Organizations | Every registered company with its plan, status and head count, with an Expiring filter; open one for its analytics, or use the Chat icon to message its admin |
| Management | Users | Managers and employees across all organisations; also the account deletion requests |
| Management | Subscriptions | The plan catalogue (Core, Prime, Elite and their durations) and every organisation's subscription |
| Management | Referrals | Customer referrals (C2C) and promoter codes with commission (B2C) |
| Management | Payments | Payments, revenue and invoice downloads |
| Management | Coupons | Discount coupons for plans |
| Management | Suggestions | Feature requests sent from the app |
| Management | Ticketing & Bugs | Support requests from organisations |
| Management | Video Hub | Guide videos shown in the app, with a second tab for who watched them |
| Management | Clickstream | How people use the app: activity, what they open and tap |
| Management | FAQ Hub | The questions and answers shown in the app's Support area |
| System | Settings | Your profile, light or dark theme, and the list of console users (super admin) |
| System | Knowledge Base | This guide |

Two items carry a small red number (a badge). The number next to **Users** is the count of account deletion requests waiting. The number next to **Suggestions** is how many suggestions the server returned (it counts the whole list, not only unread ones, so it does not go down when you read them).

Live Chat is not in the menu any more. It is the round button at the bottom right of the screen (see below).

At the bottom of the menu you see your initials, name, e-mail address and role. The log-out icon opens the **Log out of Clockyn?** box. See [Signing in](kb:signing-in).

## The top bar

The top bar sits above the page and shows the page title on the left and a group of buttons on the right.

- **Menu button** (three lines): visible on a narrow screen. It slides the left menu in and out.
- **Notifications bell**: opens a short list called **Needs your attention**. See below.
- **Theme button** (moon or sun): switches between light and dark. Settings also offers **Light**, **Dark** and **System** (follow the computer). The choice is saved on this device only.
- **Refresh button** (circular arrow): present on many pages. It reloads that page's data without reloading the whole page.
- **Your name box**: initials, name and role of the person signed in.

Some pages show a search box in the top bar. It is not the same as the search and filters above each table; use the ones above the table to find rows.

## The notifications bell

Click the bell to open **Needs your attention**. It lists only the things that have a count above zero:

- **Live chat**: unread messages. Choosing it opens the chat panel.
- **Account deletion requests**: requests waiting. Choosing it opens that page.
- **Feature suggestions**: suggestions on the list. Choosing it opens Suggestions.

If everything is zero it says **You're all caught up**. The small red dot on the bell shows only while at least one count is above zero. The chat count updates by itself; the other two are read when a page loads, so press refresh or reload the page to bring them up to date. Press Escape or click outside to close the list.

## The floating chat button

The round chat button at the bottom right is on every page except the Live Chat page itself. A red number on it is the unread message count. Click it to open the chat panel without leaving the page you are on. The panel has a **Live** or **Reconnecting** status, an **Open full page** link and a close button. On a phone it fills the screen. Press Escape to close it. Read [Live Chat](kb:live-chat) for how to answer customers.

## Patterns shared by most pages

- **Statistic tiles** at the top show totals. Many are links that open the related page.
- **Search and filters** sit above each table. Typing in the search box narrows the rows as you type (some pages wait a moment after you stop typing); drop-down filters apply at once.
- **Export** (on list pages such as Organizations, Users, Payments and Referrals): a button offers **Excel** or **PDF** and downloads exactly the rows currently shown after your filters. It needs two small libraries from the internet; if it says the export library did not load, check your connection.
- **Loading, empty and error states**: while data loads you see grey placeholder bars; an empty list says so; a failed load shows a message and a **Try again** button.
- **Messages**: small notices appear at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds ("saved", an error message from the server).
- **Confirmations**: actions that remove data show a box first. The most destructive ones ask for an e-mail code as well.
- **Phone layout**: on a narrow screen wide tables turn into stacked cards and the menu opens from the menu button.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bell shows a dot but you cannot find the item | The dot counts chat, deletion requests and suggestions together | Open the bell and choose the row |
| Numbers look old | Only chat updates live; the rest load with the page | Click Refresh or reload |
| Export does nothing | The export libraries did not load | Check your connection and try again |

## Common questions

### Where did Live Chat go?
It moved from the left menu to the round chat button at the bottom right. The full Live Chat page is still available through **Open full page**.

### Why does the Suggestions badge never reach zero?
It shows how many suggestions exist, not how many are unread. There is no read marker in the data it reads.

### Does the theme change for everyone?
No. It is saved in your browser on this device only.

### Where is the Knowledge Base?
In the left menu under System. See [Welcome](kb:welcome) for how it is organised.
