This glossary explains the terms used across the Clockyn Console and its knowledge base. Each term has its own heading. Where a topic has its own page, the definition points to it.

## Terms

### Account deletion request
A company owner's request to have the account and its data removed. Deleting is permanent and asks for an e-mail code. See [Account deletion requests](kb:account-deletion-requests).

### Active Today
A Dashboard card listing organisations with punch activity right now. See [Dashboard](kb:dashboard).

### Admin (company Admin)
The owner of a customer company in the Clockyn app (role 1 in the app). Not the same as a console super admin. See [Roles and permissions](kb:roles-and-permissions).

### B2C referral
Business-to-customer referral. A promoter such as a YouTube or Instagram channel gets a code; companies registering with it get a discount and the promoter earns commission. See [B2C referrals](kb:referrals-b2c).

### Bell
The notifications button in the top bar. It lists unread live chats, account deletion requests and feature suggestions. See [Console tour](kb:console-tour).

### Branch
A work location of an organisation. A company adds at least one branch when it registers.

### C2C referral
Customer-to-customer referral. A company shares its own code; a new company that enters it while registering gets a discount, and the referrer gets the same once the new company pays for its first plan. See [C2C referrals](kb:referrals-c2c).

### Clickstream
A record of the screens people open and buttons they tap in the app, without typed text. See [Clickstream](kb:clickstream).

### Commission
Money owed to a promoter for each company that pays a first plan: a percentage of that plan's amount excluding GST, or a fixed amount. See [Commission and payouts](kb:b2c-commission-and-payouts).

### Console
The Clockyn Console: this web site, used by Clockyn's own team.

### Coupon
A percentage discount a company can enter when buying a plan. It cannot be combined with a referral reward. See [Coupons](kb:coupons).

### Credit
When a company with a running plan switches plan, the server credits the old plan: 10 staff times its monthly price times the months it actually charged (free months excluded). See [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

### Department
A group inside an organisation, such as Sales.

### Duration
How many months a plan row lasts, for example 1, 6 or 12.

### Employee
A person who works for a customer company and punches in with the Clockyn app (role 3 in the app; the Users page calls it Employee).

### Expiring
An orange badge on the Subscriptions page for a plan that ends within 15 days.

### Face punch
Recording attendance with face verification in the Clockyn app.

### FAQ bucket
The top-level group of an FAQ in the app's Support area; each FAQ also has a category inside its bucket. See [FAQ Hub](kb:faq-hub).

### Free months
Months of a plan's duration the customer does not pay for. A 6-month plan with 1 free month charges 5 months and is valid for 6. See [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

### Free trial
A limited period before a new organisation buys a plan. All features are allowed and the staff limit is 10. Organizations shows the status **Free Trial**.

### Geofence
An area around a workplace within which punching in is accepted.

### GST
The tax added to plan prices: 18% of the amount after discounts, rounded to a whole rupee.

### Invoice
The PDF issued for a paid plan, downloadable from Payments and Subscriptions.

### Live Chat
Real-time conversations between a company Admin and Clockyn's support team. See [Live Chat](kb:live-chat).

### Manager
A person in a customer company with delegated permissions (role 2 in the app).

### Offer text
Optional wording on a plan row (up to 80 characters) shown on its card in the app; empty means an automatic "N month(s) free". See [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

### One-time code (OTP)
The six-digit code e-mailed to you to sign in; valid 5 minutes. See [Signing in](kb:signing-in).

### Operator
Anyone who signs in to the console: a super admin or Staff. See [Roles and permissions](kb:roles-and-permissions).

### Organisation
A customer company registered on Clockyn (spelled "Organization" on console pages).

### Payout
A payment made to a promoter, recorded on the promoter's detail view. See [Commission and payouts](kb:b2c-commission-and-payouts).

### Payroll
The salary feature of the app: salaries, advances and payments for staff.

### Plan
What an organisation buys: Core, Prime or Elite, in a duration. See [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

### Promoter code
The six-character code of a B2C promoter, typed by a super admin and never changed afterwards. See [B2C referrals](kb:referrals-b2c).

### Referral reward
The discount earned through a referral. Usable once, on a first plan or renewal of Prime or Elite, not with a coupon.

### Remember me
A login tick box that removes the 30-minute idle timeout. See [Signing in](kb:signing-in).

### Renewal
Extending the current plan. Always 12 months, with the same share of free months as the current plan.

### Shift
A planned working-time pattern assigned to staff in the app.

### Staff
In the app, an employee. In the console, operator role 2. The context tells you which.

### Subscription
An organisation's current plan with start date, end date and staff limit. See [Subscriptions and plans](kb:subscriptions-and-plans).

### Super admin
Console role 1: can do the sensitive actions such as changing referral discounts, recording payouts and managing console users.

### Ticket
A support request from an organisation, with the status Open, In Progress or Resolved. See [Ticketing and Bugs](kb:tickets-and-bugs).

### Tier
A plan family: Core, Prime or Elite. Core lacks staff location and CRM, Prime lacks CRM, Elite has everything. All durations of a plan share its tier.

### Upgrade (adding staff)
Raising the staff limit of a running plan. Extra staff pay the monthly price for the whole calendar months left, with no free months.

### Video Hub
The page where guide videos for the app's Support tab are managed. See [Video Hub](kb:video-hub).

### Video Views
The second tab of Video Hub: who opened which video. See [Video Views](kb:video-views).

### Void
To cancel a record without deleting it. A referral reward or a payout can be voided; it stays visible but no longer counts.

## Common questions

### What is the difference between Admin and super admin?
An Admin owns a customer company and uses the app. A super admin is a Clockyn team member with full rights in this console.

### What is the difference between a tier and a duration?
The tier decides the features; the duration decides how long one purchase lasts.

### Is "Staff" a person in a company or a console role?
Both, in different places: an employee in the app, operator role 2 in the console.

### What does "excluding GST" mean?
The amount before the 18% tax is added. Commission is worked out on it.
