The Clockyn Console is the web site that Clockyn's own team uses to run the Clockyn platform. You use it to look after the companies that use Clockyn, their plans and payments, the chats and suggestions they send, and the help content shown inside the Clockyn app. This page is your starting point: it explains what the console is, who it is for, and the order in which to learn it.

## What Clockyn does for its customers

Clockyn is a staff attendance and workforce-management app. A company uses it to record when staff arrive and leave (punch in and punch out, with face verification and location checks), to plan shifts, to handle leave and other requests, to track expenses, to run payroll, to read reports, to manage leads (CRM) and to chat with staff. Customers use the Clockyn mobile app (Android and iOS) and a web version of it. They pay for a plan, and the plan decides which features they get.

In this knowledge base a customer company is called an **organisation**. The person who registered the company and owns it is that organisation's **Admin**. Under the Admin there are **managers** and **staff** (employees). These are the people you see on the Users page of the console.

## What the console is for

The console is not the Clockyn app. It is a separate site for Clockyn's team only. With it you can:

- see every organisation, its plan, its status and how many staff it has (Organizations, Dashboard);
- look at all managers and employees across organisations (Users) and handle account deletion requests;
- manage plans, subscriptions, payments, coupons and referral codes (Subscriptions, Payments, Coupons, Referrals);
- answer customers: Live Chat, Suggestions;
- publish the help content inside the app: Video Hub (guide videos) and FAQ Hub (frequently asked questions);
- study how people use the app (Clickstream);
- manage who can sign in to the console itself (Settings).

## Who uses the console

Everyone who signs in is called an **operator**. There are two kinds. A **super admin** (role 1) can do everything, including the sensitive actions such as changing referral discounts, recording payouts and managing console users. A normal operator (role 2, shown as **Staff**) can see the same pages and do the day-to-day work but cannot do the actions reserved for super admins. Read [Roles and permissions](kb:roles-and-permissions) early: it also explains how an operator is different from a company Admin in the Clockyn app.

## Your first day

1. Ask a super admin to add you as a console user (Settings, Users, Add User) and tell them which e-mail address to use.
2. Sign in with the e-mail code. See [Signing in](kb:signing-in).
3. Read [Roles and permissions](kb:roles-and-permissions) so you know what you can and cannot change.
4. Take the [Console tour](kb:console-tour) with the console open next to you.
5. Open the Dashboard ([Dashboard](kb:dashboard)) and the Organizations page ([Organizations](kb:organizations)) and look at real rows.
6. Follow the [Daily checklist](kb:daily-checklist) for one day, with a colleague nearby.
7. Skim the [Glossary](kb:glossary) and keep [Troubleshooting](kb:troubleshooting) handy.

> **Tip** Do not change anything while you are learning. Reading, searching, filtering and exporting never change customer data. Actions that change data always show a button or a confirmation box first, and the most destructive ones (deleting an account or an organisation) ask for another e-mail code.

## Common questions

### Is the Clockyn Console the same as the Clockyn app?
No. The app is what customers use to run their company. The console is what Clockyn's team uses to look after all customers. Customers cannot sign in to the console.

### Do I need to install anything?
No. The console is a web site. Open it in a current browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and sign in.

### Who gives me access?
A super admin adds your e-mail address under Settings, Users. You then sign in with a code sent to that address. There is no password.

### Where do I get help if something does not work?
Start with [Troubleshooting](kb:troubleshooting). If it does not solve it, ask a super admin or the developers listed for your team, and say which page you were on and what the screen showed.

### Can I use the console on a phone?
Yes. The pages adapt to a narrow screen and the left menu opens from a menu button at the top. Some wide tables become cards. See [Console tour](kb:console-tour).
